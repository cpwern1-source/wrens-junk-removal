# Honesty Gates — Wren's Junk Removal

What this brand refuses to fabricate. CHARTER invariant 8 enforces this list.

## Never-generate list

- **Third-party trademarks.** No reproduced Toyota emblem, grille mark, "TACOMA"/"TRD" badging, or
  any other brand's logo on the truck. Render a generic stylized pickup. Drop license plates or leave
  them blank.
- **No real people as endorsers.** No depiction of Wren, customers, or any identifiable person
  presented as a testimonial or endorsement. Generic/abstracted figures only, if any.
- **No fake credentials or claims.** No invented awards, certifications, "#1 rated" badges, star
  ratings, review counts, or licensing/insurance claims baked into artwork.
- **No real partner/competitor brands.** No depicting other haulers, charities, or disposal partners
  without a real relationship.
- **No fabricated locations presented as real.** Generic Montana mountains are fine as motif; do not
  render a specific real landmark captioned as Wren's facility.

## Brand-text rule

Logo wordmarks ("WREN'S", "JUNK REMOVAL") that must be exactly right come from the real logo asset
(`public/logo.jpg`) — composite or set in real type rather than trusting model-rendered lettering.
Generated emblems may show *placeholder* or no text; final lockups use the real wordmark.
