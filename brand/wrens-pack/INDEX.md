## Fidelity rules

- **Canonical:** the truck's tan body color, pickup proportions, and lifted stance. The logo's flat illustrated style, color palette, and mountain motif.
- **Must NOT be model-invented:** any legible third-party branding. The Tacoma's Toyota emblem, "TACOMA"/"TRD" badging, grille trademark, and license plate must be dropped, abstracted, or replaced — render a generic stylized pickup, never a reproduced Toyota mark (see `honesty.md`).
- **Known quirk:** the reference is a real photo at a slight 3/4 angle. The logo target wants a cleaner SIDE profile — the angle is a guide, not to be copied verbatim.
